# Risks of AI

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) poses significant risks alongside its benefits. Unchecked AI development could lead to unintended consequences, such as biased decision-making or the perpetuation of social inequalities through poorly designed algorithms. Autonomous systems, from self-driving cars to military drones, raise concerns about accountability in catastrophic failures. Moreover, the misuse of AI for deepfakes, misinformation, or cyberattacks threatens societal trust and security. As AI becomes more sophisticated, it risks surpassing human control, leading to ethical dilemmas and unforeseen harm. To mitigate these dangers, it is imperative to establish transparent regulations, ethical guidelines, and robust oversight mechanisms.
